THIRD MAJOR ORIGINAL IP

Shantu

A science-fiction techno-thriller

A third major original IP from Dheere Studio, built around intelligence, technology, ambition, uncertainty, and the consequences of creating systems that may become larger than their makers.

Position Third Major Original IP
Development Concept, Character & Technology
Long-Term Vision Multi-Format Science Fiction IP
01 — THE BEGINNING
AN ORIGINAL TECHNOLOGICAL WORLD

Shantu is being built around a question: what happens when intelligence stops being only human?

The project is being developed as a major original IP within Dheere Studio's larger creative direction.

Its foundation combines science-fiction, technological suspense, character-driven storytelling, and questions about intelligence, control, responsibility, and consequence.

The deeper story architecture is being intentionally kept undisclosed while the project remains in development.

02 — A DIFFERENT IDENTITY

Built around intelligence.

Shantu belongs to the same studio as Shunyavas and OWLMAN, but its creative identity is deliberately different.

Where fantasy can build civilizations and superhero fiction can build larger-than-life identities, Shantu explores a world shaped by research, engineering, software, machines, information and rapidly changing technological possibilities.

The aim is to make the technology feel like part of the story rather than decoration around it.

03 — THE CORE IDEA
01

Intelligence

The story examines the meaning, limits and consequences of increasingly advanced intelligence.

02

Technology

Software, systems, computation and infrastructure are treated as part of the world's dramatic fabric.

03

Control

The relationship between creator, system and control forms an important conceptual layer of the project.

04 — CREATIVE FOUNDATIONS

A thriller first. A technology story underneath.

Shantu is intended to work as a cinematic thriller even for audiences who are not deeply interested in technology.

The technology provides the environment, stakes and possibilities, while the characters carry the emotional and human dimension.

The audience should understand enough to feel the danger without requiring the story to turn into a technical lecture.

01

Human

People remain the emotional center of the story, even when the systems around them become extraordinary.

02

Plausible

Technology should feel grounded enough that extraordinary events still feel believable.

03

Mysterious

Information should be revealed progressively rather than explaining everything immediately.

04

Consequential

Technological choices should create meaningful effects on people, systems and the wider world.

05 — CHARACTER & WORLD
THE HUMAN SIDE

Technology may drive the story, but people give it meaning.

Shantu is being designed so that its technological ideas are supported by characters with distinct goals, relationships, fears, ambitions and contradictions.

The surrounding world will also reflect how research institutions, startups, engineers, specialists, companies and everyday users interact with rapidly changing technology.

Much of the deeper character and plot architecture will remain part of the story itself rather than being published here in advance.

06 — CURRENT DEVELOPMENT
DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Building the foundation.

Shantu is currently being developed at the concept, character, technology and story-architecture level. The objective is to establish a strong foundation before expanding the project into larger forms.

07 — FUTURE HORIZONS

Start with the story. Let the universe grow with it.

Shantu is being developed first through formats that give the story room to establish its characters, atmosphere, ideas and world. The immediate creative focus is on the written and visual foundations of the IP.

01

Web Novel

The novel format provides the space to establish the central story, characters, technology, relationships, suspense and the deeper questions behind the world.

02

Comics

Comics can give Shantu a visual identity of its own while expanding important moments, characters, environments and parts of the world through sequential storytelling.

03

Animation

Animation can eventually bring the atmosphere, technology and character tension of Shantu into motion, while preserving the story's identity rather than simply retelling it.

04

Future Possibilities

Larger formats can be considered later, once the story and the core world are strong enough to support them. The current priority is building the foundation properly.

08 — THE WORLD OF SHANTU

A technological world that keeps moving.

Shantu is being imagined in a world where advanced technology is not isolated inside a single laboratory. It exists across institutions, companies, research environments, software systems and everyday life.

That makes the world itself important. A technological change can affect more than the people who created it. It can influence organizations, infrastructure, information, decisions and the way people understand what is happening around them.

The story will use that connected environment to create scale without revealing everything at once.

09 — STORY EXPERIENCE
HOW THE AUDIENCE SHOULD FEEL

Understand enough. Question everything else.

Shantu is intended to create a feeling of gradual discovery. The audience should be able to follow the story clearly while still feeling that there is something larger underneath the visible events.

Information, systems and character motivations should therefore be revealed with purpose. The project should create curiosity through what it shows, what it delays, and what it leaves open.

The goal is not to hide information merely for the sake of mystery. The goal is to make each reveal change the audience's understanding of what came before.

10 — THEMATIC QUESTIONS

Technology can solve problems. It can also create entirely new ones.

Beneath the thriller surface, Shantu is interested in questions that become more important as intelligence and technology become more capable.

01

Creation

What responsibility comes with building something whose capabilities may exceed the expectations of its creators?

02

Responsibility

When a technological decision produces consequences far beyond its original purpose, who remains responsible for the result?

03

Ambition

How far can people push a system, an invention or an idea before the pursuit itself becomes the danger?

11 — LONG-TERM FOUNDATION
THE APPROACH

Build depth before scale.

Shantu is not being developed around the idea of making the largest possible story immediately. The priority is to make the foundation strong enough that future expansion feels earned.

Characters, technology, rules, institutions, atmosphere and the central mystery all need to support one another. Each layer should make the world feel more coherent rather than simply making it larger.

FIRST PRIORITY Story & Web Novel
VISUAL EXPANSION Comics & Animation
LARGER FORMATS To Be Considered Later
THE LONG VIEW

Build the idea. Build the tension. Let the world discover the rest.

Shantu is still in development. Its deeper story, characters and architecture are being shaped carefully before they are revealed.

The immediate journey begins with the web novel, then opens naturally toward comics and animation. Everything beyond that can wait until the foundation deserves a larger stage.

For now, this page is only the doorway into the IP — enough to understand its identity, without giving away the experience that the story itself is meant to reveal.
