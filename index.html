What is Dheere Studio?

A small studio building original worlds, one story at a time.

Dheere Studio is an independent creative studio based in India. We create original stories and then build them out into full worlds — with their own characters, visual style, and, over time, games, animation, and interactive experiences.

We'd rather work on a small number of ideas and take them deep than publish a lot of things quickly. That's why each project gets its own history, characters, and details that make it feel like a real place — not just content.

Dheere Studio began with a simple ambition: create original stories and give them enough room to become something larger than a single format.

Behind the scenes

Questions that guide how we work.

We're a new studio, so we're still figuring a lot of this out. These are the questions we keep coming back to as we build.

01What makes a fictional world feel real?

Not piling on details, but keeping every detail consistent with the same place and history.

02What makes a character worth following?

Flaws, clear wants, real consequences, and something the reader recognizes in themselves.

03When does a story deserve to become interactive?

Only when giving people a choice or a way to explore actually adds something the original story can't.

04How much should we show on the homepage?

Enough to make you curious — the full story lives on each project's own page.

05What should we keep unfinished for now?

Anything that's still changing needs room to be reworked before we show it publicly.

About the studio

We build slower.
We think it shows.

Dheere Studio is an independent creative studio based in India, building original stories, fictional worlds, and the experiences that come out of them.

We're not saying slow is always better — just that some worlds need time to become specific and believable, instead of being rushed out.

We start with original stories and ideas, then build outward into visuals, games, animation, and interactive experiences as each project is ready for it.

What is Dheere Studio?
