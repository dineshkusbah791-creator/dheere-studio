Summary

Dheere Studio is an independent creative studio founded by Harsh Kushwah. The studio focuses on building original stories, fictional worlds, and creative experiences with a long-term approach to storytelling and worldbuilding.


Shunyavas is the first major original fantasy universe currently being developed by Dheere Studio. The project explores story, mythology, characters, lore, and the foundations of a larger fictional world.


The broader creative direction of Dheere Studio includes original stories, fantasy worlds, worldbuilding, animation, games, interactive experiences, and creative technology.
