SECOND MAJOR ORIGINAL IP

OWLMAN

The Nisaachar

A second major original IP from Dheere Studio, developed with a long-term vision for character, identity, atmosphere, and multi-format storytelling.

Position Second Major Original IP
Development Character & World Development
Long-Term Vision Multi-Format IP
01 — THE BEGINNING
AN ORIGINAL IDENTITY

OWLMAN is being built to become more than its beginning.

The project is being developed as a major original IP within the larger creative direction of Dheere Studio.

The aim is not to introduce something disposable. The foundation is being designed with enough room for the character, setting, themes, and larger identity to evolve over time.

Much of the deeper architecture remains intentionally undisclosed while the project is in development.

02 — A DIFFERENT IDENTITY

Built to stand on its own.

OWLMAN belongs to the same studio as Shunyavas, but it is being developed with a fundamentally different identity.

Its tone, atmosphere, visual language, and storytelling direction are intended to make it immediately recognizable as its own IP.

The goal is not to repeat something that already exists. It is to establish a new identity that can carry its own weight.

01

Character

A central identity designed to support a much larger narrative future.

02

Atmosphere

A distinct emotional and visual language with its own presence.

03

Identity

A recognizable original IP intended to remain meaningful beyond a single release.

03 — CHARACTER & CONSEQUENCE

A character should carry a world.

OWLMAN is being approached primarily through character.

The intention is to create an identity whose decisions, internal conflicts, relationships, and consequences can generate stories naturally.

Spectacle can be part of the experience, but it should never be the only reason the character exists.

CHARACTER

Identity

The person behind the identity matters as much as the identity itself.

STORY

Consequence

Choices should leave traces on both character and world.

WORLD

Context

The character should exist inside a believable larger environment.

TIME

Evolution

The IP should be able to grow naturally as the story expands.

04 — LONG-TERM VISION

One identity. A much larger horizon.

OWLMAN is intended to function as a long-term character and intellectual property rather than a single short-lived project.

Its foundation is being developed with the possibility of multiple stories, different creative expressions, and a larger future ecosystem around the IP.

Character A strong central identity capable of carrying stories.
Stories Room for narratives to expand over time.
World A larger setting that can become richer with time.
Expansion Potential for future forms of creative expression.
05 — CURRENT DEVELOPMENT

Still being built.

OWLMAN is currently in development. Its deeper story architecture, world, characters, and direction are being developed before broader details are revealed.

The public-facing project is therefore intentionally limited to the larger identity and ambition of the IP.

CURRENT PHASE Concept & World Development
PUBLIC STATUS Intentionally Limited
STUDIO POSITION Second Major Original IP
06 — FUTURE HORIZONS

The beginning is only the first layer.

The long-term ambition is to build an IP with enough identity and depth to explore different forms of storytelling without losing what makes OWLMAN itself.

01

Narrative

Stories can establish the character, setting, tone, and larger conflicts.

02

Visual Storytelling

Illustration, animation, cinematics, and visual media can expand its presence.

03

Interactive Experiences

Future interactive formats may allow audiences to experience the IP differently.

04

A Larger IP

The ultimate ambition is to let the character and world continue growing beyond their beginning.

THE LONG VIEW

Build the identity. Earn the mythology. Let it grow.

OWLMAN is still at an early stage. The deeper story remains intentionally behind the curtain while the foundation is developed.

For now, the important thing is the direction: this is being built as a major long-term original IP of Dheere Studio.
