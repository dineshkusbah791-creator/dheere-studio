ABOUT DHEERE STUDIO

An independent creative studio building original stories, worlds, and experiences.

What is Dheere Studio?

Dheere Studio is an independent studio focused on building original stories, fantasy worlds, and creative experiences across different forms of media.


We aren't just a blog or a writing account. The goal is to take a single story and let it grow over time into books, artwork, animation, games, and other creative formats when the time is right.

Why "Dheere"?

Dheere means slowly, but the idea isn't to move without ambition. It is about giving an idea enough time to become what it is supposed to become.


Not everything needs to be rushed out for quick attention.

"Slow does not mean lazy. Slow means deliberate."

It means taking time to think through a story, rebuild its world, and make sure the foundation is solid before sharing it with the world.

What We Create

Over time, our work takes shape in a few different forms:


  • Stories — Original novels and narratives.
  • Worlds — Characters, mythology, lore, and world rules.
  • Experiences — Animation, games, and interactive projects.
  • Ideas — Studio updates, essays, and creative experiments.

About the Founder

Dheere Studio was founded by Harsh Kushwah with the goal of building original creative projects over time rather than chasing short-term attention.


The studio is being built around a long-term vision: create strong original stories, develop their worlds carefully, and eventually explore them across different creative forms.

Where We're Starting

Our first major project is Shunyavas—an original fantasy universe currently in development.


We are starting with a story foundation because written words build the strongest base. Before a world can turn into visuals or games, its characters, history, and rules need to be clear first.

What's Next

Dheere Studio is still right at the beginning. The focus right now is simple: build Shunyavas, set up the studio foundations, and bring together a community of people who want to make things of their own.

Dheere Studio Kya Hai?

Dheere Studio ek independent creative studio hai jo original stories, fantasy worlds aur alag-alag tarah ke creative experiences banane par focus karta hai.


Yeh sirf koi writing account ya simple blog nahi hai. Maqsad ek kahani se shuru karke use waqt ke sath books, artwork, animation, games aur dusre formats tak le jana hai jab sahi time aaye.

"Dheere" Hi Kyun?

Dheere ka matlab slow hota hai, lekin iska matlab bina kisi ambition ke chalna nahi hai. Iska matlab hai kisi idea ko utna waqt dena jitna use sach mein accha banne ke liye chahiye.


Har cheez ko jaldbazi mein bahar lana zaroori nahi hota.

"Dheere hone ka matlab lazy hona nahi hai. Dheere hone ka matlab soch-samajhkar kaam karna hai."

Iska matlab hai kahani ko waqt dena, uski duniya ko ache se samajhna, aur ek mazboot foundation taiyar karna pehle se bina kisi ghabrahat ke.
