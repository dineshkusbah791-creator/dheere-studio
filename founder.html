The Founder

Harsh
Kushwah.

Founder of Dheere Studio — building original stories, fictional worlds, and creative projects with the intention of developing them for the long term.

01 — The Beginning

I did not start Dheere Studio because I wanted to create another website.

The idea was to create a place where original things could begin small and grow over time.

Dheere Studio began from an interest in stories, fictional worlds, ideas, philosophy, and the possibility of building something that could continue developing for years.

I was not interested in creating something that only existed for a short moment and disappeared when attention moved somewhere else. I wanted to begin building a foundation that could become stronger through time and actual work.

At the beginning, that foundation is naturally small. There is no reason to pretend otherwise. But small does not mean meaningless.

Some of the most ambitious things begin as a single idea, a rough document, an unfinished story, or a question that someone decides is worth exploring seriously.

Dheere Studio is my attempt to give those ideas a place to grow.

02 — Why I Started

I wanted to build something that belonged to me.

A large amount of creative work today moves extremely quickly. Ideas are often shaped around trends, algorithms, short attention spans, and the pressure to constantly release something new.

There is nothing inherently wrong with moving quickly. But I do not think speed should always decide what a creative project becomes.

My interest is in developing original intellectual property, original characters, worlds, stories, concepts, and ideas that can develop their own identity.

I do not want every creative decision to depend on what is popular for a particular month. Some ideas deserve the freedom to develop slowly until their real direction becomes clearer.

Dheere Studio gives me a structure for thinking beyond one isolated project. The studio can become the foundation behind multiple stories, worlds, and creative experiments.

01

Originality

The goal is not to reproduce something that already exists. Inspiration will always exist, but the final work should develop its own identity, perspective, characters, and direction.

02

Long-Term Thinking

Not every project has to prove itself immediately. Some ideas need time, revision, research, failure, and development before their real potential becomes clear.

03 — The Way I Think

Stories first. Then everything around them.

My interest is not limited to one medium. I am interested in the connection between stories, characters, philosophy, mythology, visual ideas, interactive experiences, and the fictional worlds that connect them.

But I do not believe that simply adding more information automatically makes a world deeper. A world can have hundreds of names, maps, systems, and pieces of lore while still feeling empty.

The foundation has to matter. Characters need conflict. Stories need direction. Worlds need history and internal logic. Ideas need a reason to exist beyond looking impressive.

That is why I prefer to begin with the core and expand outward rather than pretending the final scale already exists.

01

Story

Narrative is where emotion, conflict, characters, choices, and meaning begin. Without a strong reason to care, scale alone does not matter.

02

World

History, mythology, cultures, places, systems, conflicts, and rules give a fictional universe the depth required to feel like more than a setting.

03

Experience

When a world becomes strong enough, there may eventually be other ways for people to experience it beyond the form in which it originally began.

04 — What Drives Me

Curiosity is usually where the real work begins.

The Question

What could this idea become if I actually gave it enough time?

Building things from nothing

There is something fundamentally interesting about beginning with almost nothing and gradually creating structure, systems, stories, identities, and worlds that did not previously exist.

Understanding difficult ideas

Fantasy, fiction, philosophy, mythology, human behavior, and imagination all raise questions that are worth exploring through creative work.

Seeing how far an idea can go

A novel might remain a novel. Or its world might eventually become capable of supporting visual storytelling, games, animation, or entirely different experiences.

The challenge of learning

I am building independently, which means many things have to be learned while the work is already happening. That makes the process harder, but it also makes the work genuinely mine.

05 — The Long-Term Vision

Dheere Studio is meant to grow into more than one thing.

I do not see the studio as being limited to a single format forever. The larger vision is to create a creative foundation capable of supporting different kinds of original work.

That does not mean trying to build everything simultaneously. In fact, doing that would probably destroy the focus required to properly build the first things.

The intention is simpler: build one strong foundation, then expand through real progress.

The Goal

Build a creative foundation where different projects can develop without losing the identity of the studio behind them.

01

Original Stories

Novels, narratives, and fictional work developed from the ground up instead of depending entirely on existing universes.

02

Fictional Worlds

Larger universes built through characters, history, mythology, lore, cultures, conflicts, and internal systems.

03

Different Creative Forms

Comics, animation, visual work, interactive media, games, and other forms when a project has genuinely developed enough to support them.

04

A Creative Community

Over time, Dheere Studio should become a place where people interested in stories, creativity, fantasy, and original work can follow the journey and become part of it.

06 — Where It Begins

Every larger vision needs a first real project.

A vision is only useful when something concrete eventually comes out of it. For Dheere Studio, that beginning is Shunyavas.

First Major Universe

Shunyavas

Shunyavas is the first major fictional universe currently being developed under Dheere Studio.

The project begins with its story foundation and gradually expands through characters, mythology, history, conflicts, locations, systems, and the rules that shape its world.

The goal is not simply to announce a large fictional universe because large universes sound ambitious. The goal is to actually develop one properly and understand what that process requires.

Shunyavas is therefore not only a creative project. It is also the first serious test of the larger philosophy behind Dheere Studio: build carefully, learn through the work, and allow the world to earn its expansion.

07 — How I Want to Build

Ambition matters. Process decides whether anything survives.

I have learned that having many ideas is not the same as building something. Ideas only become useful when they eventually move through research, structure, experimentation, execution, revision, and improvement.

The process will change as I learn, but the general direction is clear.

01

Start with the core

Find the central idea before expanding outward. What is this story, world, or project actually trying to become?

02

Build the structure

Develop the characters, systems, themes, history, and internal logic required to give the idea a stronger foundation.

03

Execute something real

At some point planning has to become actual work. A project cannot remain permanently inside notes, documents, and ideas.

04

Review honestly

Not everything will work. The important thing is identifying weak decisions instead of becoming emotionally attached to every early version.

05

Improve and continue

Long-term work is not created in one perfect attempt. It develops through repeated improvement and the willingness to stay with the difficult parts of the process.

08 — The Reality

This is still the beginning.

Dheere Studio is not being presented as something that is already complete.

There is still a significant amount of work ahead. The studio, its projects, its systems, its identity, and its community all need to be built over time.

I would rather be honest about that than create the appearance of a finished institution when the real work is still being done behind the scenes.

Being at the beginning is not a weakness. Pretending that the beginning is already the final result would be.

Build before claiming.

The work should exist before the ambition around it is exaggerated.

Learn through execution.

A large part of building independently is learning by solving real problems while creating the project itself.

Improve the foundation.

Early work will not be perfect. The important part is improving the systems and the work rather than pretending the first version is final.

Stay with the work.

Long-term projects only become meaningful when they are given enough consistency to actually develop.

09 — What This Is Not

I do not want the appearance of building to replace the act of building.

01

Not an empty brand

A name, logo, and website are not enough. The real identity of the studio has to come from the work created under it.

02

Not instant success

There is no realistic reason to expect everything to become large immediately. The focus has to remain on building something worth growing.

03

Not a finished story

The direction is visible, but much of the journey has not happened yet. This page exists to show the beginning honestly.

10 — What I Want to Build

Not all of this exists yet. That is the point.

These are directions I want Dheere Studio to eventually be capable of exploring. They are not promises with fixed deadlines.

The mistake would be trying to force every possibility into existence at once. The priority is to build each stage properly and allow future possibilities to come from real progress.

01

Novels

Long-form stories with enough depth, characters, themes, and structure to stand on their own.

02

Comics

Visual storytelling that can explore worlds and characters through an entirely different creative language.

03

Animation

Moving stories into a visual form when the creative and narrative foundation is strong enough.

04

Games

Interactive experiences that allow people to enter, explore, and participate in fictional worlds differently.

05

Worldbuilding

Building fictional universes with enough internal depth, history, mythology, and structure to keep developing.

06

Philosophy & Ideas

Exploring larger questions through fiction, creative work, concepts, and the ideas that exist beneath the stories themselves.

07

Merchandise

Physical creative extensions of worlds and projects when there is something meaningful behind them.

08

A Community

Creating a space where people can follow the work, the ideas, the projects, and the journey as the studio continues to develop.

I don't know everything this will become yet.

That uncertainty is not a reason to avoid beginning. The direction is clear enough: create original work, build strong foundations, keep improving, and give the right ideas enough time to become something worth remembering.
