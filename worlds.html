The Philosophy

A World Should Feel Alive.

We don't want to create backgrounds that only exist behind the main character. We want to build worlds that feel like they existed long before the story began—and will continue to exist after it ends.

Every world can have its own history, kingdoms, forgotten civilizations, beliefs, legends, conflicts, creatures and unanswered questions.

The Architecture

A universe is built layer by layer. Some parts are visible immediately. Others remain hidden until the right moment in the story.

◈

Characters

Heroes, villains, rivals and ordinary people whose choices shape the future of the world.

◇

Mythology

Ancient stories, forgotten gods, legendary beings and mysteries that influence the present.

▲

Nations & Factions

Kingdoms, clans, organizations and groups with their own beliefs, ambitions and conflicts.

◎

The Creative Identity

Indian At Its Soul. Original In Its Form.

The worlds being created at Dheere Studio will draw creative energy from the incredible depth of Indian history, mythology, philosophy, geography and storytelling traditions.

But the goal is not to simply repeat existing mythology. The goal is to create completely original worlds with their own characters, histories, systems and identities.

Cultural Depth Ancient traditions, philosophies and storytelling ideas can inspire deeper original creations.
New Mythology New legends, creatures, civilizations and mysteries built specifically for each universe.
Global Scale Stories created from an Indian creative perspective but designed to connect with audiences anywhere.
A Distinct Identity The ambition is to create worlds that people can recognize as their own, not copies of something that already exists.
