SHUNYAVAS

An original fantasy universe currently in development.

Status: In Development

About the Project

Shunyavas is a expansive fantasy universe built around high-stakes storytelling, deep-rooted mythologies, and intricate worldbuilding. It serves as the flagship narrative IP of Dheere Studio.


Designed to bridge philosophical themes with rich dark-fantasy elements, Shunyavas explores the delicate balance between void and existence, human ambition, and cosmic balance.

Current Development

Primary Medium

Novel

The foundational story of Shunyavas is currently being written as a core web novel series that introduces the main characters, world mechanics, and overarching narrative arc.
