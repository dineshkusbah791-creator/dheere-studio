01 — THE BEGINNING

More than a single story.

Shunyavas is being developed as the flagship narrative universe of Dheere Studio. The goal is not simply to create a fantasy story with a beginning and an ending.

The larger ambition is to build a living fictional universe—one capable of carrying different stories, characters, regions, time periods, conflicts, and perspectives.

A single protagonist may begin the journey, but the world itself is designed to become larger than any one character.

02 — THE CORE
SHUNYAVAS

A world shaped by existence, absence, and everything in between.

At its conceptual core, Shunyavas explores the tension between creation and destruction, ambition and consequence, memory and history, order and chaos.

The universe does not treat power as a simple reward. Power changes people, civilizations, relationships and the direction of history. Every ability, system and conflict is intended to exist inside a larger social and philosophical context.

03 — CULTURAL FOUNDATION

Rooted in India. Built for the world.

Shunyavas is being imagined with a strong connection to the philosophical, cultural and historical richness of India.

This does not mean copying mythology directly or simply placing familiar cultural symbols inside a fantasy setting. The intention is deeper: to study the way civilizations, philosophies, belief systems, landscapes and traditions can inspire an original fictional world.

Indian storytelling has an enormous depth of thought around time, duty, consciousness, cycles, identity, destruction and rebirth. Those foundations can inspire stories that feel culturally grounded while still being completely accessible to a global audience.

04 — WORLD ARCHITECTURE

The world must feel like it existed before the story began.

A believable universe cannot be built only around the protagonist. Shunyavas is being designed with layers that can continue to reveal themselves over time.

WORLD

Geography

Landscapes, territories and distant regions will influence how people live, travel, fight and survive.

PEOPLE

Civilizations

Societies will not exist merely as visual backdrops. Their histories, systems and conflicts will matter.

POWER

Systems

Power will have structure, limitations, consequences and different interpretations.

HISTORY

Forgotten Ages

The present world may be shaped by events that its inhabitants no longer fully understand.

CONFLICT

Factions

Groups will have their own interests, beliefs, ambitions and internal contradictions.

UNKNOWN

The Unexplained

Not every mystery should receive an immediate answer. Discovery is part of the universe.

05 — POWER & ASCENT

Strength will have structure.

Shunyavas is intended to contain a deeper hierarchy of capability rather than a vague system where characters simply become stronger whenever the story requires it.

Rank, mastery, knowledge, experience and potential do not necessarily mean the same thing. A powerful individual may lack wisdom. A lower-ranked person may understand something others do not.

I

Foundation

The first understanding of ability, discipline and the limits of the individual.

II

Growth

Capability begins to develop through experience, practice and confrontation.

III

Mastery

Knowledge becomes more precise and an individual begins to understand the deeper mechanics of their path.

IV

Transcendence

The boundary between ordinary understanding and something greater begins to weaken.

The final rank architecture, terminology and progression mechanics are still in development. The objective is depth and consistency—not complexity for its own sake.

06 — PEOPLE OF THE WORLD

Characters should carry more than abilities.

A powerful fictional universe fails if its characters exist only to move the plot forward.

In Shunyavas, character development is intended to include personal history, ideology, fear, ambition, relationships, contradictions and the consequences of previous choices.

History Where they came from.
Desire What they truly want.
Conflict What challenges their beliefs.
Consequence What their choices change.

PRESENT

PAST

09 — FUTURE HORIZONS

The novel is the beginning, not the boundary.

If the foundation becomes strong enough, Shunyavas has the potential to expand beyond written fiction.

Every future format should add something meaningful to the universe rather than simply repeating the same story in another medium.

01

Illustrated Stories

Comics and visual storytelling can explore important moments and stories beyond the main narrative.

02

Animation

Selected sequences and stories may eventually take advantage of motion, sound and cinematic storytelling.

03

Interactive Worlds

Games and interactive experiences could allow audiences to explore regions and systems directly.

04

World Codex

A dedicated archive could document characters, civilizations, locations, history and deeper lore.

THE LONG VIEW

Build slowly. Build deeply. Build something that can last.

Shunyavas is still at an early stage. Much of its architecture remains under development, and many ideas will evolve, disappear or be rebuilt.

That is intentional. A universe of this scale should not be rushed into existence. The objective is to create a foundation strong enough for stories that can continue to grow over time.
